Dataset and variable description

The ert_dem dataset identifies episodes of democratization and autocratization in V-Dem datasets. Data ranges from 1990 to 2025, including more than 680 episodes of regime transformations.The data contained in this dataset will be key to determine which countries experienced regime changes and when these changes happened. With the ultimate objective being to determine whether mass mobilization leads to political regime changes, we select a series of variables from this dataset whch will be useful for our analysis:

Variables Description Type
country_id V-Dem country identifier. double (integer, [3,236])
country_text_id V-Dem country identifier in text. character
country_name Full V-Dem country name. NULL
year Year of observation. double (integer, [1900,2025])
v2x_regime The regime type for the country-year according to Regimes of the World (4 values). double (0 - closed autocracy, 1 - electoral autocracy, 2 - electoral democracy, 3 - liberal democracy)
reg_type Classifies whether the regime in that country-year is a democracy or autocracy. double (0 - autocracy, 1 - democracy)
v2x_polyarchy Gives the country’s score on the Electoral Democracy Index (EDI, V-DEM) in that year. double (interval [0,1])
row_regch_event Measures whether there was a regime change in that country-year according to Regimes of the World (not necessarily “genuine” = followed by elections) double (-1 - change from democracy to autocracy, 0 - no regime change, 1 - change from autocracy to democracy)
reg_trans Measures whether there was a “genuine” regime change in that country-year. double (-1 - democratic breakdown, 0 - no regime, 1 - democratic transition)
reg_start_year, Determines the year a regime was founded (needed for the reg_type). double (year, [1900,2025])
dem_ep Whether there is an ongoing democratic episode during the year in that country. double (dummy [0,1])

aut_ep Whether there is an ongoing autocratization episode during the year in that country. double(dummy [0,1])
aut_ep_start_year Determines the year the autocratization episode began. double (year, [1905,2025])

Cleaning the dataset and variable modification:

The rest of the variables contained in the dataset are removed. This includes: episode description variables and outcome variables, the new dataset is called ert_df_clean:

ert_df_clean <- ert_df |>
  select(country_id, country_text_id, country_name, year,v2x_regime, reg_type, v2x_polyarchy, row_regch_event, reg_trans, reg_start_year, dem_ep, dem_ep_start_year, aut_ep, aut_ep_start_year)

Note that the large portion of NA for the dem_ep_start_year and aut_ep_start_year indicate “non applicable” observations, i.e. that there is no episode associated with this particular observation.

Further, as our goal is to determine whether mass mobilization leads to changes in political regimes. Thus, we must modify the dem_ep and aut_ep variables so that they indicate the year the democratization or autocratization episode begins, as opposed to every year it lasts. These variables are particularly interesting for out analysis as they capture the movements towards or away from democratization/autocratization, without necessarily implying a regime switch (which are more rare).

ert_df_clean <- ert_df_clean |>
  mutate(
    dem_ep_new = dem_ep == 1 & year == dem_ep_start_year,
    aut_ep_new = aut_ep == 1 & year == aut_ep_start_year
  )
